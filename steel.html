About the Metal Weight Calculator

This calculator estimates the weight of common metal shapes using the dimensions you enter and an approximate material density.

How the calculation works

The basic calculation is:

Weight = Volume × Material Density

Dimensions are entered in millimetres and converted to metres before the calculation is performed.

Steel Density

Standard mild and carbon steel, including typical hot rolled and cold rolled products, are calculated using an approximate density of 7,850 kg/m³.

Galvanised Steel

Galvanised steel has a zinc coating applied to the steel. The amount of zinc can vary depending on the coating specification, so the current calculator uses the underlying steel density and treats the result as an approximation.

Stainless Steel

Stainless steel is available in many different grades with different densities. TradeCalc therefore uses an approximate density rather than attempting to represent every grade.

Aluminium

Aluminium is considerably lighter than steel. The calculator uses an approximate density of 2,700 kg/m³.

Important Information

Actual supplied weights can vary due to material grade, manufacturing tolerances, section dimensions, corner radii, coatings and other manufacturing factors.

For purchasing, engineering or structural applications, always check the manufacturer's published specifications when an exact weight is required.
